# How to play

Two black cats, brother and sister, share a house. You play one; the computer or a friend plays the other.
Whoever has
the most points when the three-minute round ends wins. Either way, both cats curl up together at bedtime.

Every number on this page comes from `config.toml` and can be changed there.

## The cats

|               | Aragorn (brother)                                 | Arwen (sister)                                                                     |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Look          | Big and round, swaggers, naps flopped on his side | Smaller, a tiny white fleck on her chest, naps in a loaf                           |
| Speed         | 2.0 metres a second                               | 3.0 metres a second                                                                |
| Claims a spot | in about 3.1 seconds                              | in about 3.1 seconds                                                               |
| Ability       | **Swat**                                          | **Stealth**                                                                        |
| Fits under    | nothing                                           | the coffee table, the dining table, the foyer bench, the closet shelf and the desk |

## Controls

| Action  | Keyboard                                 | Touch                            |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Move    | WASD or arrow keys, or click where to go | Tap where you want the cat to go |
| Ability | Space or E                               | Round button, bottom right       |
| Sound   | Button at top right                      | Same                             |

Tapping or clicking a spot sends your cat there by the shortest route around the furniture. Tap a ring
to go and sit in it. Pressing a movement key cancels the walk. On an iPhone the game works in either
orientation; landscape shows more of the house. The phone's silent switch also silences the game.

## Playing with a friend

1. One of you picks **Play with a friend**, then the cat they want to be. A room code appears, such as `K7QX`.
2. Send the friend the code, or tap **Copy invite link** and send that.
3. The friend opens the link, or picks **Play with a friend** and types the code, then taps **Join**. They
   play the other cat, and the round starts.

Whoever opened the room runs the game, so keep that page open and in front; if it closes, the round ends for
both. If the friend leaves, the computer takes over their cat. After a round, the person who opened the room
can start another. It works on most home networks; see [Multiplayer](multiplayer.md) for when it does not.

### If it won't connect

Open **Trouble connecting?** under the room code. It shows whether the game can reach its matchmaking
servers, and **Check my connection** tests your network in a few seconds. Each of you should run it. If the
advice doesn't help, tap **Copy report** and send it to whoever looks after the game. The report has no IP
addresses or room code in it.

## Scoring

| What                    | Points         | Who   |
| ----------------------- | -------------- | ----- |
| Each nap spot you hold  | 1 per second   | Both  |
| A gold treat            | 5              | Both  |
| Knocking something over | 2              | Both  |
| A mischief-list item    | 15             | Both  |
| Hiding near Aragorn     | 0.5 per second | Arwen |

### Nap spots

There are nine, each marked with a ring: the sunbeam, the fireplace, the couch, the bed on the counter, the
food bowl, the warm dryer, the shag rug, the big bed and the kid's bed.

- **Claiming:** sit still inside a ring and it fills with your colour. Moving stops the fill, and the fill
  drains away if you leave.
- **Holding:** a spot you own keeps paying whether or not you stay on it, until the other cat claims it.
- **Contested:** if both cats sit on the same ring, nothing happens until one leaves.
- **Colours:** orange is Aragorn, blue is Arwen, white is free. The dots under the clock show all nine.

### Treats

Six gold treats sit around the house: in the kitchen, under the dining table, in front of the TV, in the
foyer, in the walk-in closet and in the kid's bedroom. Walk over one to eat it; it comes back 20 seconds
later. The one under the dining table is where only Arwen can reach.

### Knocking things over

A coffee cup on the counter, the TV remote on the covered table in front of the fireplace and a toy mouse on the living-room floor. Brush past one and it
ends up on the floor. Someone puts it back after 25 seconds, so it can be knocked again.

## Abilities

### Aragorn: swat

- Hits Arwen if she is within 1.3 metres, whichever way he is facing.
- A hit stuns her for 1 second, then she bolts for 1.5 seconds. He can swat again after 4 seconds.
- A miss only costs 0.7 seconds.
- It cannot hit her while she is under furniture or in stealth.

### Arwen: stealth

- She is unseen for 3 seconds and cannot be swatted.
- She can use it again 9 seconds after she vanished.
- She can still claim spots, eat treats and knock things over while unseen.

### Arwen: hiding

Under the coffee table, the dining table, the foyer bench, the walk-in closet's shelf or the kid's desk she
cannot be swatted, and Aragorn cannot follow.
While she is hidden within 3 metres of him she scores for teasing him.

## Mischief lists

Each cat has five extra goals, shown at the top left. Each is worth 15 points, once.

| Aragorn                       | Arwen                                  |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Swat Arwen                    | Hide near Aragorn for 5 seconds        |
| Knock the cup off the counter | Sneak the treat under the table        |
| Claim the couch               | Knock the remote off the covered table |
| Bat the toy mouse             | Nap in the sunbeam                     |
| Hold 4 spots at once          | Take the bed on the counter            |

## The house

The main floor of the real house, laid out from the floor plan and dressed from photos. It is one floor, about
11 by 12 metres:

- **Den** (north-west): the dining table, the balcony sliding door with a sunbeam, and the blue cat tree.
- **Living room** (centre): one long room with the fireplace, the TV above it, curtained windows, a plaid-covered
  table in front of the fireplace, and a sectional couch and wicker rocker along the far wall.
- **Kitchen**: an island whose counter is backed by a half wall, which turns into a full-height pillar wall
  with a walkthrough to the living room.
- **Laundry room** (south-west): washer, dryer and pantry.
- **Foyer**: the front door, and a railed stairwell down to the basement, which is not built.
- **Bedroom wing** (east): the primary bedroom, a hall, the walk-in closet, the primary bath, a second bath
  and the kid's bedroom.

The garage is only bare concrete outside the walls. Cats can hop onto the couch, the rocking chair, the covered table, the floor
cushions, the island and its half wall, both perches of the cat tree, the washer and dryer, and both beds.
